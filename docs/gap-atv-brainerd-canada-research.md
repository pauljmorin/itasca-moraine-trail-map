# ATV trails, Brainerd to Canada — research

**Date:** November 13, 2026
**Scope:** the wider corridor Brainerd → Grand Rapids → Ely → Voyageurs / Boundary Waters → Canada.
Counties: Crow Wing, Cass, Itasca, St. Louis, Lake, Koochiching, plus Aitkin (Soo Line / Blind Lake
feeder from the south) and Cook (eastern / Gunflint approach). Beltrami, Hubbard, Lake of the Woods
and Roseau systems are listed as adjacent context only, not counted.
**Companion file:** `gap-atv-brainerd-canada-inventory.csv` (one row per trail/system).
**Prior file (not redone):** `gap-atv-competition-research.md` + `gap-atv-trails-inventory.csv`
(Arrowhead reroute corridor, Grand Rapids→Ely). This report carries its St. Louis / Lake / Cook
figures forward, adds the Brainerd end and the north end, and corrects one double-count in it
(see §1). Where a figure below duplicates the prior file it is cited to the prior file, not
re-derived.

**Method:** DNR A–Z OHV listing read in full November 13, 2026
(https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/ohv/az.html); DNR per-trail detail pages and 2026 GeoPDFs; county ATV
maps and county board records; club sites and the ATVAM regional club map; county ordinances
retrieved as PDFs. Authoritative sources only for trail existence and mileage — a DNR or county
publication, or a club's own map, counts; a forum post or a retailer's trail list does not.
Where two authoritative sources disagree, both figures are given and the authoritative one named.

**Correction to the prior file.** The prior file's corridor-core total (~1,083) double-counted the
Iron Range OHV State Recreation Area: it carried both a "main unit 36" row and a "West Unit 36"
row. The DNR A–Z lists two units — West Unit 36 and **East Unit 13**. The "main unit" row is the
West Unit under another name. Corrected Iron Range total is 49 (36 + 13), corrected St. Louis
total 465, and the prior file's core total corrects from ~1,083 to **1,059.5 miles**
(1,083 − 36 duplicate + 13 East Unit). The wider-corridor 1,630.5 above = corrected core
1,059.5 + Crow Wing 186.5 + Cass 166 + Aitkin 89.5 + Blue Ox 74 + Voyageur Country 36 +
Rabey Line 19. **Convention:** the totals count DNR A–Z OHV rows as published, including the
three rows that are not ATV — Miller Hills 14 (OHM-only), Alger Grade 13 (OHM-only) and Mesabi
Mountain 4 (ORV-only) — each flagged wherever it appears. The A–Z is the authoritative
designated-trail inventory and the prior file used the same convention. ATV-only, the wider
corridor is 1,630.5 − 14 − 13 − 4 = **1,599.5 miles (~1,600)**.

**Designated miles are not the whole story.** Three different numbers describe the same ground
and they must not be added together:
- **Designated (DNR A–Z / GIA) miles** — the ~1,630.5 above. A trail is "designated" when it
  appears in the DNR A–Z listing or a county GIA sponsorship list.
- **Club-system miles** — what a club actually maintains and signs, including forest roads,
  county-road rights-of-way and undesignated connectors. The Voyageur Country club system is
  ~200–250 miles against 36 designated miles (§5); the Northwoods Regional ATV Trail Alliance
  claims 500+ miles against the Soo Line corridor (§8).
- **Everything legally rideable** — DNR's January 29, 2025 reply to the Minnesota Chapter of The
  Wildlife Society totalled **101,417 miles** statewide, including unsigned forest-access routes.
  That figure is a different universe from designated trail and is not used here.

Systems share tread in places (Prospector Loop ties into Lake and Cook systems; Taconite ATV
segments are also snowmobile trail; the Blue Ox is also a snowmobile trail in winter), so the
1,630.5 contains some double-counted tread. It is an inventory sum, not a surveyed network length.

Notes:
- The 186.5 counts Emily–Blind Lake whole (it spans Aitkin + Crow Wing). Wholly-in-Crow-Wing
  mileage is 170.5.
- **Miller–Black Bear discrepancy:** DNR A–Z says 31 miles; Explore Minnesota describes a
  "20-mile network." DNR is authoritative; the 20-mile figure is probably a subset or an older
  figure.
- **Mississippi River Northwoods discrepancy:** 11 (A–Z table) vs 11.5 (DNR detail page and the
  county's 2016 designation record — County Board designated 11.5 miles June 14, 2016, 10 miles
  inside the Mississippi River Northwoods property; GIA-funded maintenance by Cuyuna Iron Range
  Riders, county Land Services oversight). The 11.5 is the better figure; the A–Z rounds down.
- **Fort Ripley maintainer conflict:** the DNR detail page names Central Lakes ATV Club; the DNR
  GeoPDF credits Balsam Trail Blazers. Both clubs are real; which holds the current GIA contract
  was not established. Do not assert a single maintainer.
- **Why Crow Wing has no DNR-built trails:** Minn. Stat. § 84.8045 bars the DNR from permitting
  land it administers in Cass, Crow Wing and Hubbard Counties to be used or developed for trails
  primarily for off-road vehicles, except on legislative approval or in designated ORV use areas.
  (https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/84.8045). Crow Wing's trails are therefore
  county/club GIA trails on county, state-forest and private land, not DNR-built trail.
- DNR trail designation order, March 2008: 14.5 miles ATV/OHM designated in Crow Wing County
  (files.dnr.state.mn.us crowwing_trails.pdf) — superseded by the current seven-trail system.
- A 2016 LSOHC correspondence records DNR + Crow Wing County + ATVAM + local clubs resolving
  the OHF-land use question: limited designation, trails closed to OHV unless posted open.

## 6. Blue Ox Trail — 74 miles, the spine to the north

Source: DNR A–Z listing + DNR detail page, https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/ohv/trail_detail.html?id=5
(read Nov 13, 2026).

- **74 miles, old railroad grade**, black spruce / swamps / upland aspen. "Provided by Koochiching
  County and maintained by the Northome Ridge Riders" — but the same DNR page's contact list says
  "Funkley to Big Falls: Brian Dreher / Northome Ridge Runners, 218-244-6821" and "Trail start to
  Funkley: Tim Foss / Timber Riders ATV, 320-296-2266." The club's legal name is Ridge Runners
  Snowmobile Club Inc (501(c)(4), EIN 411597063, Northome; 2024 revenue $130,034); ATVAM lists it
  as "Ridge Runners Snowmobile/ATV Club, NE-40, Northome." DNR's "Northome Ridge Riders" is a
  variant spelling on the same page — treat "Ridge Runners" as the club name.
- **County split (Itasca County ATV map, 2018/2023): 23 miles in Beltrami, 11 in Itasca, 40 in
  Koochiching.** The DNR A–Z lists the county as Koochiching only — do not assert county from the
  A–Z row. The southern end is near Funkley (Beltrami County); the trail runs Northome → Big Falls
  → Littlefork, ending at the north and south edge of Littlefork and connecting via road ditch on
  Highway 217. Littlefork permits ATV travel through town on city streets, alleys and county road
  rights-of-way.
- **Four maintenance sections** (Itasca County map): (1) DNR Parks and Trails – Bemidji
  (David Schotzko, 218-308-2367); (2) Northome Ridge Runners ATV Club; (3) DNR Parks and Trails –
  Grand Rapids; (4) DNR Parks and Trails – Tower (Joe Majerus, 218-300-7841).
- Season: Big Falls north 5/15–11/30; Funkley north 5/15–11/30; Funkley south 5/15–11/1.
  Closed at Big Falls near the Bigfork River, Highway 6 to County Road 31. OHMs not allowed south
  of Funkley or on county road rights-of-way. Parking at Big Falls (Hwy 6 / Hwy 71) and Northome
  (Hwy 46 / Hwy 71).
- The Blue Ox is the only designated ATV trail that physically connects the Itasca systems to the
  Koochiching / Voyageur Country systems. Everything north of it to Canada is proposed, not built
  (§9).

## 7. Voyageur Country ATV Trail — 36 designated miles, ~250-mile club system

Sources: DNR A–Z listing; Voyageur Country ATV Club site, https://www.voyageurcountryatv.org/;
City of Orr, https://orrmn.gov/activities/; Rainy Lake tourism,
https://rainylake.org/thing-to-do/voyageur-country-atv/; Crane Lake Township minutes, March 13,

- **Designated: 36 miles** (DNR A–Z; ATV 36, OHM 36). The A–Z lists the county as "Lake" —
  **wrong**: the Phase 2 EAW and all club materials place the system in Koochiching and St. Louis
  Counties. Do not assert county from the A–Z row. "Provided by St. Louis County and maintained by
  the Voyageur Country ATV Club" (City of Orr / Crane Lake tourism).
- **Club system: ~200–250 miles.** 2018 Crane Lake minutes: "largest ATV Club in Minnesota at
  +700 members"; "Completed mapping and signing +200 miles of ATV corridor routes and trails
  between Sheep Ranch Road, Orr, Cook, and east to Crane Lake and Echo Trail – printed 5000 maps

  **250 miles of ATV trail**." The system "uses a combination of forest roads, wooded trails, and
  county road rights-of-way to connect the communities of Cook, Crane Lake, Buyck, Camp 5 township,
  and Ely" and "ties into the existing Prospectors Trail system, and it will tie into the Quad
  Cities trail system when it is built from Pfeiffer Lake to County Road 361." Stops include
  Vermilion Falls, Pelican River overlook and the new Vermilion River trail bridge.
- The 36 and the 250 describe different things (designated GIA trail vs signed corridor routes).
  Only the 36 is in the 1,630.5 total.
- Club: Voyageur Country ATV Club Inc, based in Crane Lake, "covers a huge area of amazing trails
  from International Falls and Orr to Ely and Cook." 2026 meeting schedule on the club site
  (January 14 Echo Trail Tavern, Buyck … November 18 The Dam, Orr). Annual Spring Ride May 2, 2026,
  Goldmine Pit. ATVAM map NE-52.
- Master Plan (2018, ~99% complete per the 2018 minutes): "identifies trail alignments from
  International Fall to Ely and from Kabetogama Lake to Lake Vermilion, looping through northern
  St Louis County and northeast Koochiching County." The 2018 master plan proposed ~538 miles in
  phases (Phase 1 ~387 miles) — a proposal figure, not a trail.
- Closest access toward Canada today: Sheep Ranch Road, ~40 miles south of International Falls
  (Rainy Lake tourism). The gap from there to the border is the subject of Phase 2 (§9).

## 9. County ATV road ordinances — what the corridor counties allow

The designated-trail mileages above are only part of the rideable network: in every corridor
county except Lake and Koochiching (see below), ATVs may also run on county roads under a county
ordinance enacted under Minn. Stat. § 84.928, subd. 1(k)
(https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/84.928). These road miles are NOT in the 1,630.5 total —
no county publishes a rideable road-mileage figure — but they are what connects the designated
trails into systems (the Voyageur Country "250 miles" is mostly this). The ordinances differ in
ways that matter for routing:

| County | Ordinance | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| Crow Wing | Ord. No. 0601 (amended 2022) | Class I + II ATVs on **all county roadways**, right shoulder or extreme right-hand side; ≤30 mph (20 mph on CSAH 66). Posted on the Cuyuna Iron Range Riders site, https://cuyunaironrangeriders.com/2023/04/11/all-terrain-vehicles-in-county-road-right-of-ways-in-crow-wing-county/ |
| Cass | Ord. No. 2024-01, effective May 16, 2024 | Class I + II on county highways, far-right side of the paved/gravel surface (NOT ditches); Class I ≤30 mph, Class II ≤ posted limit; Board may close segments. https://mn-casscounty.civicplus.com/DocumentCenter/View/3065/ATV-Ordinance-2024-01-PDF. ## 11. Lobbying / advocacy organizations

**ATV Minnesota (ATVAM) — the statewide association.** 73 member clubs; 501(c)(4); est. 1983;
President Ron Potter. What it publishes for this corridor: the **regional club map**
(https://atvmn.org/images/REGIONAL_MAP_15886.pdf) and the member-clubs directory
(http://www.atvmn.org/atvmn/member-clubs) — these map **club locations, not trails**, and carry
no trail mileage. Do not cite the ATVAM map as a trail-mileage source. Legislative positions:
April 2, 2025 letter supporting **SF 593 and SF 594**, bipartisan bills providing funding from
the state's ATV Dedicated Account for trail development, construction and maintenance
(https://www.lrl.mn.gov/archive/minutes/senate/2025/envlegacy/20250403/envlegacy_20250403_SF0594-ATV-MN-ltr.pdf).
ATVAM opposes HF 1012 / SF 1245 (prior file). History claimed by the association: instrumental
in the 1985 statute allowing ATV use on frozen lakes, ditch lines, select state forest roads and
trails, and establishing the Grant-in-Aid trail fund. Funding context: ATV Dedicated Account,
Minn. Stat. § 84.927; $60 / 3-year registration. The north-of-Highway-2 rule (Minn. Stat.
§ 84.777, cited in the prior file) governs ATV operation north of US Highway 2 — the whole
northern half of this corridor sits under it.

**Northeastern Regional ATV Joint Powers Board — closes prior-file gap §9.11.** Organized August
2021 "with the goal of creating a regional ATV authority to coordinate planning, permitting,
design, and construction activities of the various ATV trail system networks throughout
Northeastern Minnesota." Board: commissioners from **St. Louis, Koochiching and Lake Counties**;
chair St. Louis County Commissioner Paul McDonald; fiscal agent St. Louis County Auditor (Joint
Powers Agreement, County Board Resolution 21-363, June 8, 2021). Meets 3rd Monday monthly, 2 pm,
St. Louis County Public Works Facility, Virginia; ATV Advisory Committee (18 ATV clubs and other
stakeholders) meets the same day at 3:30 pm. Draft Master Plan exists (50 MB, hosted by SEH).
Board page: https://www.sehinc.com/online/northeastern-regional-atv-joint-powers-board.
Current work (Koochiching County Board minutes, Dec 17, 2024 and Sept 8, 2026): extend the trail
from Kabetogama to approximately Ray, then all the way to International Falls, and connect to the
Blue Ox Trail. **University of Minnesota Extension study, August 2024** (Brigid Tuck and John
Bennett, for the board): "Economic contribution of ATV trails in Koochiching, Lake, and St. Louis
Counties" — 986 usable survey responses; 2023: an estimated **219,230 riders** used ATV trails in
the three counties; riders contributed an estimated **$36.1 million** in economic activity;
trail development and improvements generated an estimated **$16.8 million**.
https://www.sehinc.com/hubfs/Website/Online/northeastern-regional-atv-joint-powers-board/Economic-contribution-of-ATV-trails-in-NE-MN-FINAL.pdf.

**Trail alliances (Minnesota Wheelin', "Strength in Numbers,"**
https://www.minnesotawheelin.org/index.php/wheelin-buzz/features/105-strength-in-numbers**).**
Clubs whose trails connect partner into alliances: Prospector Trail Alliance; **Northwoods
Regional ATV Trail Alliance** (began as Aitkin County clubs; the 2007-legislated 70-mile project
to connect Aitkin to Itasca Counties; "in 2024, this trail system has grown to over 500 miles,
using the Soo Line North and South corridor trails to connect ATV trails in Cass, Crow Wing,
Morrison, Mille Lacs, Pine, and Carlton Counties"; 9 clubs — a system figure that overlaps the
designated miles in §§2–4, NOT additive); NorthStar Trail Alliance; **Itasca County ATV Trail
Alliance** (umbrella for Itasca's eight clubs). The Joint Powers Board "represents 18 clubs,
with a vision to grow the region, rather than separate, disconnected trails."

**2025–2026 funding (county board records).** St. Louis County, June 9, 2026: FY27 IRRR Culture
