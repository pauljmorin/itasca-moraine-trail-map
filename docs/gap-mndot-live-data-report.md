### Speed limits — not found anywhere

NO posted speed limit figure was found for any of the seven segments. TDMS,
EMMA and the four 2024 Keypoints reports do not list speed limits (the word
"speed" does not appear in any of the four Keypoints texts). The statutory
rural default was NOT substituted — posted limits remain unverified and would
need a MnDOT speed-zone order or a field sign check. **Do not quote 55 mph
as the posted speed for any segment.**

### Road / surface width

The Keypoints reports do NOT print a traveled-way width per keypoint. The
numeric column next to the surface type is the Statutory (Legislative) Route
number per the report legend (e.g. MN-6 = 34, MN-210 = 36, US-59 = 30,
I-94 = 392) — not a width. Surface type shown is Bituminous on all segments
above (Concrete only on I-94).

## What could not be verified

- **Posted speed limits for all seven segments** — not listed in TDMS, EMMA,
  or the 2024 Keypoints reports; no MnDOT speed-zone source was found in the
  time available. Do not infer them.
- **Shoulder widths for three spots:** (a) the US-75 Red River bridge
  segment — the Wilkin Keypoints report ends at the Clay/Wilkin county line
  (mi 233.70); (b) US-59 through Fergus Falls including the Pelican River
  crossing — an 11.1-mile keypoint gap (mi 211.54–222.67) in the Otter Tail
  report; (c) CR-88 from Rothsay to the North Dakota border — the Otter Tail
  CSAH 88 report ends at the Wilkin County line (mi 16.38) and the Wilkin
  report contains no route 88, so the border stretch is not a single "CR-88"
  and its shoulders are not covered by these four reports. These are gaps in
  the reports, not zero-shoulder findings.
- **CR-88 as a single 39.5-mile route to the border is not supported by the
  county reports:** CSAH 88 in Otter Tail Co. is 16.38 miles (Fergus Falls to
  the Wilkin line); past Rothsay the route to the border is a zigzag of other
  Wilkin county roads and then US-75 from Kent. AADT and shoulder figures for
  the mid-stretch / border part of that 39.5-mile walk therefore could not be
  attributed to "CR-88" and are not given.
- **MN-169 "8-ft shoulders" (Eagles Nest) is not confirmed as a uniform
  figure;** Keypoints 2024 shows predominantly 4 ft paved in Eagles Nest
  Township. The 2018 rebuild itself is confirmed. The viewer's Street View
  point text for Eagles Nest has been corrected accordingly, and the "55 mph
  statutory default" wording on the US-75 Kent point has been removed.
- MN-135 703 (2025) is an EMMA map label only, not popup-verified; CR-88
  AADT labels near Rothsay and the MN-210 East 5,200 (2015) and 3,770 (2025)
  labels are likewise label-only.
- Point 7's panorama is 238 m from its point and point 5's is ~20 m south —
  both on the same road, offsets stated above. Point 11 has no panorama at
  the point.
- The ArcGIS "Outside Shoulder Width" service returned in one search was
  checked and rejected — a sample query returned Florida counties (Collier,
  Miami-Dade), not Minnesota, so it was not used. No files were edited by the
  browser pass itself.
