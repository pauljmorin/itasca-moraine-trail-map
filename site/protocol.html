Trail data collection — volunteer protocol

Itasca Moraine Chapter, North Country Trail. For the 2–4 of us collecting trail data with an iPhone or an Android phone. Brief, and in order.

Before you go out

  • Install Geo Tracker (by Ilya Bogdanovich) — free on iPhone and Android. It records your walk as a track file. Your phone’s camera does not record location in photos, so the track is how we place each photo on the map.
  • Install the Google Drive app and sign in with your Google account.
  • Getting access to the upload folder. The chapter shares the photos-inbox folder with you by your Google email, with Editor access so you can upload into it. Inside it is a subfolder named with your photographer code — your initials (for example, PJM). That is where your photos and track go. If you don’t have a Google account or can’t use the Drive app, tell the chapter and they will send you a simple upload-form link instead.

In the field

  1. Start Geo Tracker recording before you start walking or photographing. Keep it running the whole walk.
  2. Photograph each thing you find with your phone’s regular Camera app — signs, posts, boardwalks, puncheons, bridges, bollards, benches, kiosks, shelters, campsites, latrines, registers, water sources, anything built or placed on the trail.
  3. For each thing, take a front photo, a back photo if there is anything on the back, and a close-up of any text, numbers, or damage. Those get combined into one pin on the map.
  4. Keep your phone’s clock on automatic. The map matches each photo to the track by time, so the clock has to be right.
  5. If you can’t tell what something is, photograph it anyway and note it — don’t guess. We’ll mark it unclassified and sort it out later.

When you get back

  1. Stop Geo Tracker and save/export the track (a GPX file).
  2. Open the Google Drive app. Go to the chapter’s photos-inbox folder, then into the subfolder named with your photographer code (your initials). Upload your original photos and the GPX track file there.
  3. Upload the originals, straight from the Camera app. Do not send photos by text, chat, or the share button — those copies lose the timing information the map needs to place them, and we can’t use them.

What happens next

  • The chapter looks at each photo group and classifies it — what it is, what condition it’s in — using the classification page. Each thing gets its own number (like ITM-CP-001) so we can track it.
  • If a photo can’t be identified, it’s marked unclassified and flagged for a walk-through — someone will go look at it in person.

The short version

  • Do: Geo Tracker on before you start. Regular Camera app. Front, back, close-up. Originals + GPX to your Drive subfolder.
  • Don’t: send photos by text, chat, or share. Don’t rename photos. Don’t guess what something is — photograph it and flag it.
