How to use
  1. Work through the pins one at a time — the photos of one thing are shown together.
  2. Pick the type and condition, add the photographer's initials and any notes, then press Confirm this pin.
  3. Your work is saved in this browser as you go, so you can stop and come back.
  4. When you are done, press Export classifications and send the downloaded file to Muse.
  5. If the grouping is wrong, use Fix the grouping below the photos — split photos out into a new pin, merge this pin into another, or correct a photo's role (front / back / detail). The pipeline only guesses the grouping; you decide.

Fix the grouping

These photos were grouped by time and place. If two different things got lumped together, split them apart. If one thing got split across two pins, merge them. Photos that arrived here by a merge can be split out again after undoing that merge.

Tick the photos to split out, above.

What is this?

Photos are the chapter's own trail photos. Serial numbers are assigned separately and are not editable here. Classifications are stored only in this browser (local storage) until you export — nothing leaves your computer on its own. Splits, merges and photo roles are included in the export so Muse can apply them to the registry.
