Itasca Moraine Chapter — Trail Map

Working draft — not an official NCTA or National Park Service map. A project of the Itasca Moraine Chapter of the North Country Trail Association, built from public data for chapter planning.

Chapter sections: 73.2 mi adopted of the 76.2 mi of trail in the official depiction. One stretch — 2.9 mi at the west end, in Itasca State Park — has no adopter (shown in red).

Purple: Laurentian Lakes Chapter, shown for context.

Data sources

  • Trail line: North Country Trail Association, on behalf of the National Park Service (official public depiction)
  • Parcels: Minnesota Geospatial Commons (MnGeo) compiled county parcels; Hubbard County open data (Hubbard is not in the state compilation). The parcels layer is currently disabled on this map.
  • Access lines: Minnesota DNR — state forest roads, state park trails & roads, OHV trails, state trails, snowmobile trails, hunter walking trails
  • Roads: Minnesota DNR / MnDOT road centerlines
  • Sections & gaps: the chapter's adoption tracker and segment boundary points, joined onto the official line
  • Waypoints (parking, camping, shelters, water, points of interest) and the Find index: North Country Trail Association public point data, plus the layers above
  • Base map — Topo (default): OpenTopoMap (CC-BY-SA); map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, SRTM
  • Base map — Streets: © OpenStreetMap contributors
  • Base maps — USGS Topo 1:24k and Aerial (NAIP imagery): USGS The National Map
  • Base map — Hillshade: Minnesota Geospatial Image Service (MnGeo / Minnesota DNR), derived from 1 m LiDAR. The terrain view — the moraine itself; recommended for reading the land.
  • Base map — Aerial: Esri World Imagery

Adopted sections show placeholder adopter names (Test adopter 1, 2, …) — the chapter's real adopter names are kept in its working files and never appear on this map. Parcel owner names and addresses, as published in the county parcel records (public record), will appear in parcel popups if the currently disabled parcels layer is re-enabled.
