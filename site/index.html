Itasca Moraine Chapter — Trail Map (working draft)

Working draft — not an official NCTA or National Park Service map. A project of the Itasca Moraine Chapter of the North Country Trail Association, built from public data for chapter planning.

Chapter sections: 73.2 mi adopted of the 76.2 mi of trail in the official depiction. One stretch — 2.9 mi at the west end, in Itasca State Park — has no adopter (shown in red).

Data sources

  • Trail line: North Country Trail Association, on behalf of the National Park Service (official public depiction)
  • Parcels: Minnesota Geospatial Commons (MnGeo) compiled county parcels; Hubbard County open data (Hubbard is not in the state compilation)
  • Access lines: Minnesota DNR — state forest roads, state park trails & roads, OHV trails, state trails, snowmobile trails, hunter walking trails
  • Roads: Minnesota DNR / MnDOT road centerlines
  • Sections & gaps: the chapter's adoption tracker and segment boundary points, joined onto the official line
  • Waypoints (parking, camping, shelters, water, points of interest) and the Find index: North Country Trail Association public point data, plus the layers above
  • Base maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors; OpenTopoMap (CC-BY-SA)
  • Shaded relief & spring aerial base maps: Minnesota Geospatial Image Service (MnGeo / Minnesota DNR) — hillshade derived from 1 m LiDAR; Spring Aerial Imagery Program 2013–14 leaf-off colour photography
  • Aerial & shaded-relief base maps: USGS The National Map (NAIP imagery, shaded relief); Esri World Imagery

Parcel popups show the owner's name and mailing address as published in the county parcel records (public record). Adopted sections show placeholder adopter names (Test adopter 1, 2, …) — the chapter's real adopter names are kept in its working files and never appear on this map.
